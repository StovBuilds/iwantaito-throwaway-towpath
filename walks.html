Millpond to Lock Keeper's Cottage

Walked 20 September 2026

Starts
Millpond Bridge
Finishes
Lock Keeper's Cottage
Distance
3.5 miles
Time
About 1½ hours

Flat, wide and easy underfoot, with swans at every bend. The path is well surfaced all the way, and the tearoom by the last lock does a very good flapjack.

  • Pushchairs
  • Pub at the end

The Long Pound

Walked 6 September 2026

Starts
Hatherley Wharf
Finishes
Ashby Bridge
Distance
6 miles
Time
About 2½ hours

A long, quiet stretch with no locks at all, so it's very peaceful. There's nowhere to buy a drink on the way, so take water. The path can get muddy after rain.

  • Dogs
  • Peace and quiet
  • Boots in winter

Starts

Finishes

Distance
4.5 miles
Time
